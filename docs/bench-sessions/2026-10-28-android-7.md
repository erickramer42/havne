# Android phone session 7: Bluetooth with no adapter

**Date:** 2026-10-28  **Phone:** Android 16 (SDK 36), the test phone
**Adapter:** none: a pair of paired earbuds (classic Bluetooth) and an
unpaired LED light (BLE) stood in, neither an ELM327
**QtTune build:** d424370 (CI run 40779962224), installed over `adb`
after the previous build was uninstalled; minimum SDK 31,
`BLUETOOTH_SCAN` with `neverForLocation` (read from the APK with
`aapt`)
**Evidence:** the owner's notes from the phone, the ELM327 wire logs
and `adb logcat`, copied off with `adb`

Step 3 of [ADR-0028](../adr/0028-app-fed-streams.md), the phone's
Bluetooth: [android-phone-runsheet.md](android-phone-runsheet.md),
Phase 6.

## Findings

1. **The permission is asked once, when Adapter opens**, not at launch:
   Android's Nearby devices prompt, worded "Allow QtTune to find,
   connect to, and determine the relative position of nearby devices?"
   (Android's text for the group, whatever the app declares). Allowed.
2. **The paired list** showed the phone's paired devices as "Bluetooth,
   paired · <BT address>", the earbuds among them.
3. **Scan for BLE** read "Scanning…" for 8.0 s (the scan's start and
   stop in `logcat`), and several "Bluetooth LE · <BT address>" rows
   joined the list, the LED light among them.
4. **Picking a row seemed to do nothing.** A row fills the URI field at
   the top of Settings, which on a phone is scrolled out of view, and
   the row showed no sign of being chosen; the first tries never
   reached Connect, so nothing was logged. Fixed after the run (below).
5. **Classic Bluetooth, the earbuds:** they offer an SPP service (many
   do, for their companion app). The RFCOMM socket connected in 0.4 s,
   core sent `ATZ`, nothing answered, and after 5 s the connect failed
   with "the adapter's driver refused: the adapter on `bt:<BT
   address>` didn't answer ATZ within 5 s"; the socket closed. About
   5.4 s from the tap to the error.
6. **BLE, the LED light:** the GATT connection came up, the MTU went to
   247, and every service was discovered. None was on ADR-0028 §2's
   list, so the last rule chose the first service with a notifying and
   a writable characteristic (a vendor's 128-bit service), switched its
   notifications on and wrote `ATZ`. Nothing answered; after 5 s the
   same refusal, `ble:<BT address>`, and the GATT connection closed.
   The light twice, and a second BLE device once, each the same.
7. **The app stayed responsive** through every connect; the activity
   log held "Connecting:" and "Connect failed:" for each.
8. **Bluetooth off, Refresh:** "Bluetooth is off: turn it on, then
   Refresh", as written, but in the dim gray of a hint, easy to miss.
   Fixed after the run (below).
9. The optional step (Nearby devices refused in Android's settings) was
   not run.

Each wire log (`ecuio-elm327.*.log`) holds the URI, `> ATZ`, "no line
and no prompt for 5 s (ATZ)", the refusal and "closed": the app-fed
stream carried core's bytes out on both kinds of link.

## After the run

- A device or recording row, or a preset, that fills the field scrolls
  the page back to it when it's above the view, and the row whose URI
  is in the field is outlined (`QmlTest.settings`,
  `test_aPickedRowScrollsTheFieldIntoView`, checked against a mutation).
- The Bluetooth line takes the warning color when it asks for
  something (Bluetooth off, the permission refused, a failed scan,
  no Bluetooth); "the scan found no BLE adapters" and the hints stay
  dim.

BENCH prep #24 is partly confirmed: the prompt, the lists, and both
links opening and carrying the setup through the app-fed stream. An
adapter's answers, the requests, and a dropped link reopened are the
MX+'s run on the car (ADR-0028 step 4).
