## Findings

1. **It installs and launches.** Play Protect asked "Play Protect
   hasn't seen this app before. Install anyway?"; the first page drew
   near instantly; every page opened from the tab bar, and turning the
   phone laid the pages out again.
2. **Settings is wider than the screen, so no session can start.** In
   portrait the URI field runs off the right edge and Connect is drawn
   past it; several rows run off too, and the page scrolls only up and
   down. In landscape the sidebar takes its share and Connect is still
   off the screen. Reproduced on the desktop at 412 × 915 logical px
   (the same build's QML): the URI row, the vehicle row, Presets,
   Profile and the Developer mode line all overflow. A `RowLayout`
   never lays out narrower than its items' natural widths (the URI
   field's is its long placeholder), and the page's `Flickable` is as
   wide as the page and clips.
3. **A white bar along the bottom covers the content.** In landscape
   the Home page's Connect sits under it and can't be pressed. Android
   15 and later draw an app targeting SDK 35 or above under the
   system bars, and the app applies no inset for them.
4. **Home doesn't fit a landscape phone.** Its column is centered on
   the page with nothing to scroll, so the logo's top is cut off
   (reproduced at 915 × 412).
5. **No zoom on a phone.** Zoom is Ctrl + / Ctrl − or Settings'
   Display size buttons, which sit in the overflowing page; there is no
   pinch.
6. The tab bar's labels are small, but every tap landed.

Phases 2–5 didn't run: nothing could connect. BENCH prep #21 is
refuted in part (installs and launches; the layout fails at phone
size); #22 and #23 are still open.

## If it fails, suspect

| Symptom | Suspect |
|---|---|
| "App not installed" | A previous QtTune signed with another key: uninstall it first |
| It closes at launch | Copy `adb logcat -d` around the launch if `adb` is at hand; otherwise note the Android version and stop |
| A blank or black window | The graphics backend: note the phone's chip if known, and stop |
| "no answer within 2 s" on WiFi with mobile data off | The phone isn't on the VX's network, or the VX's network side hung (power-cycle it) |
| "the network is unreachable" or a permission error on WiFi | `INTERNET` missing from the manifest, which CI checks: copy the error whole |
