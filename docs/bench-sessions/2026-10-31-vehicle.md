## Findings

1. **The VX reads the codes on a real ECU.** Ignition off, every
   diagnostics item went unanswered and the line said so; once the ECU
   woke, the unanswered items were asked again on the same connect and
   the report filled in (07:58:51). It shows what the MX+'s report
   showed the same morning.
2. **Live hid exactly what the bitmaps predict:** 81 of 130 signals.
3. **The session logs were lost** to a test run on the same laptop,
   as the teardown warns. The request bytes for this session are the
   MX+'s.
4. **Data stopping** is an operator's observation without evidence;
   it stays open until a session with its log shows it.
