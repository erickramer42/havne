# ELM327 BENCH session: the VX over WiFi, no bus

**Date:** 2026-10-27, 21:51:29–21:52:14  **Adapter:** OBDX Pro VX, as an ELM327, over its WiFi access point
**Machine:** the dev machine, joined to the VX's network ("OBDX Pro VX <MAC>", open, 2.4 GHz); the VX on USB power from the laptop
**QtTune build:** the WiFi change, uncommitted on top of 8fa2e22
**Evidence:** the connect's wire log and the recording's `events.log`
(Record sessions on), in
[2026-10-27-elm327-bench-4/](2026-10-27-elm327-bench-4/)

## Purpose

BENCH prep #20: `elm327://` over TCP ([ADR-0026](../adr/0026-elm327-transport.md)'s
appendix "WiFi over TCP") on a real adapter for the first time.
`elm327://192.168.4.1:23?profile=raw&poll=0x0C` with no bus, about 45 s,
then disconnected.

## Findings

1. **It connects and the setup goes through.** The wire log's header
   reads "192.168.4.1:23 over TCP". `AT Z` answered `OK` alone, `AT I`
   `ELM327 V2.1`, every contract command `OK`, `STI` `?`, `AT RV`
   `0.00V`: the answers of its USB port
   ([2026-10-27-elm327-bench-2](2026-10-27-elm327-bench-2.md)). The
   setup took 235 ms, each prompt 5 to 39 ms after its command, where
   USB's took 0.1 to 3.8 ms. The "Session:" line read "ELM327 adapter
   (ELM327 V2.1); ISO 15765 at 500000 baud; profile raw, channel
   0x7E8/0x7E0 (rx/tx); battery 0.0 V".
2. **No bus is quiet, as on USB.** All 146 requests answered `NO DATA`:
   the first three 108 to 130 ms after the request, every later one
   305 to 332 ms (median 306.5). That is USB's 100 then 300 ms, the
   three frames the VX's CAN controller holds with nothing to
   acknowledge them, plus a few ms of WiFi. The link went quiet
   ("vehicle not responding") 2.5 s after the connect, with no episode;
   USB's went quiet at 2.4 s.
3. **Nothing came unasked,** and nothing failed: no reopen, no silence.
4. **The close:** "the session closes", then "192.168.4.1:23 closed".

Not exercised: leaving the VX's network and rejoining it, and WiFi
while a program holds the VX's USB port.
