# Android phone session 13 — a sleep in session time (BENCH prep #27)

**Date:** 2026-10-29, 17:59–18:22  **Phone:** Android 16 (SDK 36), the test phone
**Adapter:** none: `mock://demo`, at the desk
**Havne build:** `27592cf`, CI run 40969094431's Debug APK (session time
on `ecuio_clock_us`, [ADR-0033](../adr/0033-session-time-counts-sleep.md));
installed after an uninstall of the v0.3.3 release, whose settings were
backed up first
**Evidence:** the owner's notes, each recording's `events.log` and
`frames.csv`, and `adb logcat` (all kept on the laptop, not committed)

Phase 8a of [android-phone-runsheet.md](android-phone-runsheet.md):
whether a sleep of the phone's CPU shows in session time as a gap, with
an "Asleep for N s" line, now that core's clock counts sleep
(`CLOCK_BOOTTIME` on Android).

## Findings

1. **On USB the CPU never slept** (17:59:28–18:08:23, the phone plugged
   into the laptop, charging, with `adb` attached). Logcat has no
   silence over 2 s in that span, and the recording's 534.54 s by
   session time match its 534.54 s by the wall clock. No "Asleep"
   line, which is right: nothing slept. #27 is untested by this run.
2. **Both gaps on Live were the app's process frozen,** not a sleep.
   Home for about 2 minutes: Android froze the app at 18:00:47.698
   (`ActivityManager: freezing`) and thawed it at 18:01:53.112, the
   recording's frames 65.4 s apart across it. The screen off for about
   5 minutes: the phone vendor's own app freezer, separate from
   Android's, froze it 3.3 s after the display went to `DOZE_SUSPEND`
   (`FZ … reason: LEV`, 18:03:51.178) and thawed it as the screen came
   on (`UFZ … reason: TopApp-ScreenOn`, 18:08:01.722), the frames
   250.6 s apart. Each gap's ends match the freeze and thaw lines
   within 15 ms. `mock://` holds no foreground service, so a freeze is
   the behavior ADR-0029 accepts; a gap on Live and in the recording
   says it.
3. **The Delivery line shows a freeze's shape.** 4375 frames, none
   dropped, the longest wait for the GUI thread 234 ms, but one value
   waited 250,666 ms to reach the model: it was staged just before the
   freeze and drained after it. The worker and the GUI thread stopped
   together, so no frame waited.

## Unplugged

The same, with the phone off USB so the CPU can suspend: `mock://demo`,
Record sessions on, then Home and the screen off for about 10 minutes
(18:10:40–18:21:55).

4. **The CPU slept, and session time counted it.** The activity log
   has thirteen "Asleep for N s: nothing was read meanwhile" lines,
   from 2.3 s to 342.1 s, 583 s in all of a 674 s session. The
   session's 674.47 s by session time match its 674.46 s by the wall
   clock; on a clock that stops in sleep it would have read about
   91 s. Logcat is silent over 2 s 33 times in the session, 590 s in
   all: the CPU suspended.
5. **Each sleep is a gap of its length, on Live and in the recording.**
   `frames.csv` has thirteen gaps over a second, one per "Asleep"
   line, each its N and about 0.3 s more (the awake moments around the
   suspend), and `signals.csv`'s `host_us` jumps at the same thirteen
   places, 608.9 s in all. The owner saw the gaps on Live.
6. **A freeze and a sleep together are told apart.** Two gaps are
   longer than their sleep: 357.5 s with 342.1 s asleep, and 122.0 s
   with 114.9 s. Each is a freeze by the vendor's app freezer (`FZ …
   reason: LEV` at 18:13:08.438, thawed by `DeviceIdleOFF` at
   18:19:05.955; again at 18:19:15.978, thawed as the screen came on at
   18:21:17.882), during which the CPU slept most of the time. The
   "Asleep" line gives the sleep alone, and the gap the whole.
7. **Delivery:** 1333 frames, none dropped, the longest wait for the
   GUI thread 106 ms; a value waited 357,633 ms to reach the model,
   staged before the freeze and sleep and drained after them.
