## Phase 3c: more than one ECU

10 s per URI: `?profile=raw&poll=0x00` (the `01 00` bitmap) and
`?profile=uds-generic&did=F190&identify=0&diagnose=0`, each physical
(`&filter=0x7E<n+8>&txid=0x7E<n>`) and functional
(`&filter=<rx>&txid=0x7DF`).

| Request | Physical (to 0x7E0-0x7E7) | Functional (0x7DF, heard on 0x7E8-0x7EF) |
|---|---|---|
| `01 00` | 0x7E0: 299 answered, every 30.7 ms; 0x7E1: 339, every 28.7 ms; 0x7E2-0x7E7: none (93-97 timeouts each) | 0x7E8: 310, every 26.6 ms; 0x7E9: 351, every 26.6 ms; 0x7EA-0x7EF: none |
| `22 F190` | 0x7E0: 136 answered, every 81.9 ms; 0x7E1: `7F 22 31` once (refused, then not asked); 0x7E2-0x7E7: none | 0x7E8: none, 10 timeouts in 10 s; 0x7E9: none, 96 timeouts |

- [x] The same two ECUs as through the MX+: 0x7E0/0x7E8 and 0x7E1/0x7E9
      answer the bitmap, physically and functionally, and nothing else
      answers
- [x] Functional requests run at the full rate through the VX (the MX+
      ran them at half: no response-count digit for a functional
      request, ADR-0026)
- [ ] **F190 through 0x7DF never completes** (a finding, below): the
      engine ECU answers it physically, but a functional request gets
      ten timeouts in 10 s, each taking about a second, where a silent
      ID takes the 100 ms wait. F190 is a segmented reply, and the
      session's `FLOW_CONTROL` filter names the request's ID: the
      adapter sends the Flow Control on 0x7DF, and ISO 15765-4 has the
      ECU wait for it on its physical ID, 0x7E0
- Evidence for v0.4.0's design: the engine ECU and a second ECU, both
  answering OBD-II requests

## Findings

1. **Packing after an ignition cycle:** the unanswered requests while
   the ECU was off tripped the three-failure fallback, for the rest of
   the session. Fixed after the run: the fallback lasts until the link
   recovers (ADR-0039's appendix, amended 2026-11-01). Phase 4 on the
   car is left.
2. **A scanned DID's late reply:** fixed after the run, a positive reply
   naming a scanned DID left unanswered now finds it (ADR-0020's
   appendix, amended 2026-11-01). The two blocks on the car are left.
3. **A functional request with a segmented reply through J2534** never
   completes: the `FLOW_CONTROL` filter's flow-control ID is the
   channel's `tx_id`, 0x7DF for a functional request, so the adapter's
   Flow Control goes on 0x7DF. Open; it reads as the cause but no
   capture of the bus shows it.
4. **`havne-cli` didn't end** when the antivirus held the surrogate at
   its first spawn: the handshake's 8 s window should have failed the
   connect. Open.
5. **`PassThruOpen` after another program used the VX** failed until the
   VX was unplugged: the vendor's state, not Havne's. Noted for the run
   sheet's "If it fails".
