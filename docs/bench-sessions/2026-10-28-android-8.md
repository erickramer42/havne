## Findings

1. **Ignition off (7a).** The RFCOMM socket connected 2.4 s after the
   tap and the setup ran as through the laptop: "Session: ELM327
   adapter (ELM327 v1.4b, STN2255 v5.12.2); ISO 15765 at 500000 baud;
   profile obd2, channel 0x7E8/0x7E0 (rx/tx); battery 12.0 V" (12.1 V
   through the laptop). Nothing answered the probe ("no answer to the
   supported-PID query") and the link went quiet 1.6 s after the
   connect ("vehicle not responding").
2. **Ignition on, the start, idling (7b), one connect of 123 s.**
   - "battery 11.5 V" (11.6 V through the laptop), then `ECU 0x7E8:
     supports 53 PIDs; 10 requests, 1 dropped: 01 5C (not in its
     supported PIDs)`, as through the laptop.
   - The identity line answered and refused the same items as through
     the laptop (the same 7 DIDs refused `7F 22 31`). Scrubbed here;
     the wire logs show its shape. A new vehicle was saved as
     "Vehicle 1" (ADR-0027).
   - The start: RPM past 300 at +18.7 s, a flare to 1,450 rpm, idle
     about 890 rpm. Battery Voltage (PID 0x42) dipped to 8.8 V in the
     crank and settled at 13.6–13.7 V. No link event through the
     crank. Coolant 28 → 58 °C over the run.
   - 4,430 frames at 36.1 a second, none dropped; no gap in RPM longer
     than 0.7 s.
   - Screenshots: Live through the start
     ([live-start.jpg](2026-10-28-android-8/live-start.jpg)) and the
     Dashboard at idle
     ([dashboard-idle.jpg](2026-10-28-android-8/dashboard-idle.jpg)).
3. **The rate (7b).** `?poll=0x0c` for 15 s: 539 replies, **36.0 a
   second**, above the laptop's ~32/s through the same adapter. The
   socket took 3.7 s to connect this time.
4. **A Bluetooth drop (7c) recovers on its own.** Bluetooth off at
   18:32:55.5: the stream failed at once ("the port failed (reading the
   reply to 01051)") and "Link: adapter fault" the same moment. The
   reopens failed "Device is powered off" on the backoff (0.1 s
   doubling to 3.2 s). Bluetooth on at 18:33:05.6; the next reopen at
   18:33:06.9 connected in 1.9 s, the setup ran, and "Link: recovered"
   at 18:33:09.9: **4.2 s from Bluetooth on to values** (about 6.5 s
   through the laptop), with no reconnect. The phone's link to the
   car's own Bluetooth came back beside it.
5. **The app in the background (7d) breaks the link, and the
   recovery is slow.** This extends Known Issue #37 to the Bluetooth
   link:
   - Home at 18:34:19.4. The request written at that moment never
     went out, and after 5 s core closed the link ("no line and no
     prompt for 5 s (01051)"). Its reopen at 18:34:24.5 timed out
     ("the link didn't open within 10 s"). The app's Bluetooth link
     does its work on the GUI thread (ADR-0028's appendix of step 3),
     which Android pauses in the background: nothing core asked of it
     ran until the app returned, and the bridge, on the same thread,
     logged "Link: adapter fault" only on return (18:34:42.6).
   - Back at 18:34:42.5, the queued calls ran at once: the stale
     write, the close, and two opens, whose two socket connects
     started together and both failed; a third connected at once
     (18:34:42.9). The stale request's reply arrived on the
     new link ("unasked: 7E803410571", dropped as unasked), and the
     adapter didn't answer the setup's `ATZ` within 5 s.
   - The next socket connect blocked for 25 s (until the link to the
     adapter timed out, 18:35:13.2), past core's 10 s wait, so core's
     next open started a second connect beside it, which blocked for
     20 s; a third connected at 18:35:19.8. "Link: recovered" at
     18:35:21.0: **38 s after the app returned**, a 62 s gap in the
     values.
   - The car's own Bluetooth dropped and reconnected the phone in the
     same minute (18:35:04.8 to 18:35:09, then failed audio connects
     until 18:35:16.5). Through 7b the phone held both links at full
     rate, so the car's link isn't the cause; whether its reconnect
     slowed the adapter's connects can't be told from one run.
6. **A short pause is harmless.** Back pressed during the rate run hid
   the app for 0.7 s (18:32:08.9): values reached the model up to 1.1 s
   late, and the link didn't notice.
7. **The Dashboard header names a Bluetooth adapter by its address's
   tail** ("Live · …<BT address>"), so the address reaches screenshots;
   the device list's name ("OBDLink MX+") would say more and give away
   less.

Socket connects took 0.5–3.7 s, and the setup after them about
1.3 s, the first `ATZ` answering in 0.9 s.

  faults within 5 s, and its recovery on return took 38 s.
