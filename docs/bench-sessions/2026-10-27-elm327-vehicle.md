## If it fails, suspect

| Symptom | Suspect |
|---|---|
| No `COMn` after pairing, or "no such port" | The wrong row: the Outgoing port is the one to open |
| "COMn: the device behind the port didn't answer" | The MX+ not powered (the Power light), or paired to the phone |
| "didn't answer ATZ within 5 s" | The Bluetooth link not up: unpair, pair again |
| Values in Live but none the VX had, or implausible ones | Copy the activity log whole; compare with the 2026-10-25 sheet |
| Quiet with the engine running | The receive filter or the header: copy the activity log; try `?poll=0x0C&txid=0x7DF` |
| The app crashes | Abort as in the vehicle sheet, and save the activity log |

## Findings

1. **The MX+ reads the car** - the first ELM327 read of a vehicle. Paired over
   Bluetooth as an outgoing COM port (COM6), connected in ~2.7 s, and ran the
   full `obd2` session through a cold start.
2. **Setup and identity.** `AT Z` ... `AT ST 19` all `OK`; session line "ELM327
   adapter (ELM327 v1.4b, STN2255 v5.12.2); ISO 15765 at 500000 baud; ...;
   battery 12.1 V" with the ignition off (15:43:58), the VX's Phase 0 reading;
   11.6 V with the ignition on (15:47:26), not comparable with Phase 0. It is
   an STN2255. Parameter 2A at default (no "set not to wait" note). Identification
   returned the same items as 2026-10-25 through the VX (VIN, CALID, CVN, ECU
   name, software number, VIN F190; the same 7 DIDs refused `7F 22 31`),
   scrubbed from the record and the wire logs.
3. **Decode matches the VX.** Engine-off PIDs and, through the cold start
   (~1400 rpm) and idle, RPM / coolant / IAT / MAF / throttle / load tracked the
   cluster (the operator's check in Phase 2; the cluster wasn't photographed),
   as the VX's values did on 2026-10-25 (#14). Battery 13.5 V at idle. In the
   wire logs every reply prints as `7E8` (three hex digits, no length digit),
   and the multi-frame ones (the VIN, the CALID, the ECU name, the DIDs)
   reassembled into the identity line.
4. **Rate.** Over Bluetooth the MX+ sustained ~32 completed requests/s, on both
   the single-PID poll (0x0C) and the full rotation - faster than the VX's 26/s
   through J2534 (2026-10-25). The STN's turnaround, not the PID count, sets it.
5. **Bluetooth drop recovers.** Bluetooth off -> the port failed at once
   (15:53:21.575, the last reply 31 ms before) and "Adapter fault" showed within
   0.1 s, not the 10 s the sheet allowed for a silent link. The reopens failed
   "no such port" on the backoff until Bluetooth was back; COM6 reopened at
   15:53:48.6, the setup ran again, and the first value came at 15:53:49.5,
   with no reconnect. Bluetooth on to the first value wasn't timed: at most
   ~6.5 s (the reopen before failed at 15:53:43.0, then the 5 s backoff and
   the setup). Most of the 28 s from fault to recovery was Bluetooth off.
6. **DID scan** (`uds-generic`, F180-F1FF): 2 found, as through the VX.
7. **VX in ELM mode** on the same car: in Phase 5, connected green, all
   pages showed values as expected (the operator's note; that connect's wire
   log wasn't kept). Later the same day, 17:06-17:13, with wire logs
   ([vx_elm/](2026-10-27-elm327-vehicle/vx_elm/)):
   - `elm327://COM7` failed writing `AT Z` after 2.1 s, and a J2534 connect
     at 17:07:19 was refused, `PassThruOpen` status 34, the VX's refusal when
     its J2534 side isn't available (ADR-0018).
   - `elm327://COM8`, ignition on, engine off (17:10:14, 24 s): `AT Z` `OK`,
     `AT I` `ELM327 V2.1`, every contract command `OK`, `STI` `?`, `AT RV`
     11.66 V. The probe and identification answered as through the MX+
     (the same 7 DIDs refused `7F 22 31`).
   - `elm327://COM8` through an engine start (17:10:44, 138 s): the crank at
     17:10:54 dipped the module voltage (PID 0x42) to 9.7 V with no link
     drop; idle ~840 rpm (peak 1058), up to 13.6 V, coolant 57 to 71 C.
     Every reply prints as `7E8`, and the multi-frame ones reassemble. ~30
     completed requests a second (the VX read 26/s through J2534). The
     engine off at 17:12:57: `NO DATA` from then to the close.

## Consequences

- The ELM327 transport is confirmed end to end on a real car, through a
  Bluetooth STN (the MX+) and a USB adapter (the VX in ELM mode, ~30
  requests a second). ADR-0026 step 5 is done.
- The MX+ sustains ~32 req/s over Bluetooth, above the VX's 26/s: recorded in
  ADR-0026 and bench-prep.
- Follow-ups: STATUS.md (step 5 done, Vehicle tier), testing.md (Vehicle tier,
  and the Win32 port now also exercised over a Bluetooth COM port), bench-prep
  #12-#17 for the MX+, and an ADR-0026 dated appendix (the rate and the drop).
