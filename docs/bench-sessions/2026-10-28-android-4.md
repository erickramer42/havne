## Findings

1. **The icon shows** on the launcher: the tachometer on the purple
   tile.
2. **The fold buttons' arrows are boxes.** Presets, Recent recordings
   and Adapters fold and open, but the arrow after each title (▸, ▾)
   draws as a box with an X: Android's fonts have no glyph for them.
3. **The bands after a turn are dark, and still there.** Upright at
   launch the app fills the screen; after turning on its side and back,
   bands stay at the right and the bottom, now the dark window
   background rather than white. The "Display:" lines (sizes in
   logical px, at 3.75x):

   ```
   14:09:57.075 Display: screen 384 × 832 (portrait, 3.75x), available 384 × 832, window 384 × 832 at 0, 0, safe area left 0, top 0, right 0, bottom 0
   14:11:18.295 Display: screen 832 × 384 (landscape, inverted, 3.75x), available 832 × 384, window 816 × 336 at 0, 0, safe area left 0, top 0, right 0, bottom 0
   14:11:26.665 Display: screen 384 × 832 (portrait, 3.75x), available 384 × 832, window 368 × 784 at 0, 0, safe area left 0, top 0, right 0, bottom 0
   ```

   The screen and its available area follow the turn; the window
   doesn't. After each turn it is the available size less 16 × 48,
   exactly the desktop's opening size in `Main.qml` (`available − 16`,
   `available − 48`, under 1280 × 800), which was bound to the screen.
   At launch Android shows the window filling the screen whatever its
   size, so the bands appear only once a turn sets the bound size.
   On its side (`…142843`) the app stops about 180 px above the bottom
   and about 130 px from the right of the full-size screenshot.
4. **A replay isn't recorded, and the Record switch stays ticked.**
   The owner asked whether it should untick.
5. **Crowded traces are thinner** but still "don't look great".
   Upright (`…142838`), nine traces through the 2nd-gear limiter:
   lambda, the ninth signal, reuses the first color (RPM's blue) with
   a dash, and its 1 px dashed zigzag between lean and rich hatches
   the chart's whole height for the limiter's 5 s; MAF, throttle and
   load fuzz along the top over the same span. On its side the same
   hatch, wider.

Not reported this run: the navigation bar's color, the connected
status line's wrap.

## After the run

- The window fills a phone's screen and follows it through a turn; a
  desktop's opening size is set once (finding 3).
- The fold buttons draw their chevron rather than a glyph (finding 2).
- The Record switch keeps its setting, the next live session's, and the
  line beside it reads "Replays aren't recorded" while the field holds a
  replay (finding 4).
- A crowded trace's stretches that swing back and forth by a tenth of
  the chart's height or more draw as a faint band from the lowest to
  the highest value per 2 px column, edged thin; its ramps, steps and
  small wiggles stay a line. The palette has ten colors, so the mock's
  lambda is solid lime (finding 5).
