## Findings

1. **The layout fix holds, but not at this phone's width.** Home lays
   out on its side and Settings' rows wrap, but in portrait, with
   `elm327://192.168.4.1:23?profile=raw&poll=0x0C` in the field, only
   "Dis" of the Disconnect button showed: the page still ran past the
   right edge. On the desktop at 320 px the longest preset buttons run
   past it, the caption beside them leaving them too little width; a
   long URI alone doesn't squeeze the button there.
2. **The system bars' area is white.** The UI keeps out of it, but a
   white strip shows where Android's navigation bar sits: on the right
   upright, at the bottom on its side (thicker).
3. **The Dashboard's dials are sized to the page's width.** On its
   side, beside the sidebar, a third of the tachometer fits;
   reproduced on the desktop at 840 × 340, where the page is about
   500 px wide and one dial a row is taller than the page. Upright the
   gauges are large for a phone.
4. **`mock://` streams, and a session outlives the background.** With
   the app in the background or the screen off for about 30 s the
   session stayed connected and the values moved again on return, but
   Live's chart has a gap for that time and the activity log says
   nothing: Android pauses the app's GUI thread (Known Issue #37).
5. **A recording is written, but can't be replayed from the phone.**
   The "Recording:" line reads
   `Documents/QtTune/recordings/20261028-103559`, which is
   `/storage/emulated/0/Android/data/org.qtproject.example.qttune/files/Documents/QtTune/recordings/20261028-103559`
   (found over `adb`): 337 frames and 2835 values, none dropped, over
   about 17 s. The replay failed: the keyboard capitalized
   `Replay://` ("no transport"), the long path was error-prone to type
   ("malformed URI"), the activity log's text can't be selected to
   copy, and **Open folder** offered an "Open with" list with no file
   manager.
6. **The phone reaches the VX over WiFi** (BENCH prep #22). Three
   connects, each running the whole setup as on the computer (`AT Z`
   `OK`, `AT I` `ELM327 V2.1`, `STI` `?`, `AT RV` `0.00V`), the prompts
   7–63 ms after their commands (the computer's 5–39 ms); green, then
   amber "Link quiet"; each ended with QtTune closing the socket. With
   mobile data on, it connected the same way: Android sent the socket
   over the WiFi network. Typing the URI on the phone was tedious and
   error-prone.
7. **The VX hands a late reply to the next connection.** The second
   connect's first `AT Z` was answered `NO DATA`, followed unasked by
   `OK` and a prompt: the first session's last request was answered
   after its socket closed, and the VX gave that answer to the next
   client. The setup's second `AT Z` ran clean (BENCH prep #20).
8. **Where the files land** (BENCH prep #23): the wire logs in the
   app's cache, `/data/user/0/org.qtproject.example.qttune/cache`
   (Android's `TMPDIR`); the preferences in
   `files/settings/QtTune/QtTune.conf`; recordings as in 5.

## If it fails, suspect

| Symptom | Suspect |
|---|---|
| "App not installed" | A previous QtTune signed with another key: uninstall it first |
| It closes at launch | Copy `adb logcat -d` around the launch if `adb` is at hand; otherwise note the Android version and stop |
| A blank or black window | The graphics backend: note the phone's chip if known, and stop |
| "no answer within 2 s" on WiFi with mobile data off | The phone isn't on the VX's network, or the VX's network side hung (power-cycle it) |
| "the network is unreachable" or a permission error on WiFi | `INTERNET` missing from the manifest, which CI checks: copy the error whole |
