# ELM327 BENCH session — the VX over WiFi, leaving its network

**Date:** 2026-10-27, 22:04–22:13 and 22:18–22:26  **Adapter:** OBDX Pro VX, as an ELM327, over its WiFi access point
**Machine:** the dev machine on the VX's network ("OBDX Pro VX <MAC>", open, 2.4 GHz); the VX on USB power from the laptop
**QtTune build:** ddc107a
**Evidence:** two connects' wire logs and their recordings' `events.log`
and `events-2.log` (Record sessions on), in
[2026-10-27-elm327-bench-5/](2026-10-27-elm327-bench-5/)

## Purpose

BENCH prep #20's second item: leaving the VX's network mid-session and
rejoining it ([ADR-0026](../adr/0026-elm327-transport.md)'s appendix
"WiFi over TCP"). `elm327://192.168.4.1:23` (the `obd2` profile: the
probe, identification and the rotation, all `NO DATA` with no bus).

## Findings

1. **QtTune's side works as the appendix says.** Leaving the network
   fails the read at once; the reopens follow the backoff; once the
   adapter is reachable and answering, the next reopen runs the setup
   and the session goes on with no reconnect.
2. **The VX's WiFi didn't come back on its own.** After the machine
   left uncleanly (its socket closed while off the network, so the VX
   never saw the close), a rejoined machine got a TCP connection whose
   `AT Z` went unanswered, and later no address from the VX's DHCP.
   Outside QtTune it was the same: no ping, no TCP connect. Only a power
   cycle brought it back, and then the same session recovered with
   nothing pressed, so QtTune wasn't what held it. Which part of the VX
   holds on (the old connection, its access point) isn't visible from
   here, nor whether QtTune's reopens every few seconds keep it held: a
   fresh connect after an unclean leave, with QtTune disconnected,
   would tell.
3. **The dev machine's Wi-Fi didn't hold the link either:** it dropped
   once unasked, with nothing changed. The laptop is the better machine
   for the WiFi items.

## Findings from both runs

- **An unclean leave hangs the VX's network side**, twice out of
  twice: the machine leaving with the socket open, by walking off the
  network or by turning Wi-Fi off. Afterwards the VX answers no ARP, no
  TCP and (in the first run) no DHCP, with QtTune disconnected as much
  as with it retrying, until it is power-cycled. QtTune's reopens don't
  cause it.
- **A clean close doesn't:** the stall at 22:18:54 ended in a close the
  VX received, and the next connect worked at once.
- **QtTune recovers whenever the adapter does,** across a stall, the
  adapter's own restart, and a power cycle, with no reconnect.
