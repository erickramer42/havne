## Findings

1. **The screen off with QtTune in front: no stall.** One connect from
   22:54:31: the screen off from 22:54:40 to 22:59:58 (three stretches,
   the lock screen between, QtTune in front throughout), then Home from
   23:00:09 to 23:02:24. **21,163 frames over 481 s with no gap over
   1 s**, the session clock in step with the wall clock (no sleep), and
   the reporter silent: the GUI thread never went 2 s without its
   heartbeat. At the screen-off the window dropped the Inactive exposes
   that android-10's deadlock drew for. The owner saw no gap on Live
   after either.
2. **Bluetooth off with the screen on crashed the app.** At 23:02:32.8,
   back in QtTune, Bluetooth off from the quick settings. A request's
   write failed ("Error while writing"), and 0.2 s later the GUI thread
   died on a null pointer: `QBluetoothSocket::errorString()` called
   from `BtLink::write`. A failed write emits `errorOccurred` before it
   returns, and its handler reports the loss and drops the socket, so
   the socket `write` went on to ask for its error text was gone. In
   android-8 and android-10 a read noticed the loss first. The app
   closed with the session's recording unfinished (its frames intact,
   no "Delivery" or "end" line).
3. **Bluetooth off and on with the screen off (a second connect,
   23:03:12):** off at 23:03:22.4, the fault at once; on at 23:03:35.8;
   one socket connect from 23:03:38.7, connected in 2.4 s; "Link:
   recovered" at 23:03:42.5, **6.7 s from Bluetooth on to values**. The
   Delivery line: 641 frames, none dropped, the longest wait for the
   GUI thread 27 ms. The owner saw the gap while Bluetooth was off, and
   data flowing again as soon as it was back.
