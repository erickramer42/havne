## 9a: Service 01 through the relay

Each run `havne-cli stream "elm327://127.0.0.1:35000?poll=0x0C"`, the
engine off (RPM 0):

| Run | Frames | Ended | Timeouts | Latency p50 / p99 / max | Interval p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1, 30 s | 945 | `seconds` | 0 | 20 / 115 / 122 ms | 29 ms |
| 2, 30 s | 940 | `seconds` | 0 | 18 / 115 / 146 ms | 31 ms |
| 3, 60 s | 1,892 | `seconds` | 0 | 18 / 106 / 143 ms | 31 ms |
| 4, Stop relaying at 13.9 s | 433 | (stopped, below) | 1 | 18 / 106 / 142 ms | 31 ms |

1. **A relayed session is an ELM327 on a socket to core.** Each
   `session` line names the MX+'s firmware, protocol 6 at 500 kbit/s and
   the engine ECU on `0x7E8`; no frame was dropped in any run. The rate,
   about 31 requests a second, is the MX+'s own over Bluetooth
   ([ADR-0026](../adr/0026-elm327-transport.md)'s appendix "the first
   vehicle session" of 2026-10-27: ~32), so the relay and `adb forward` add no limit of
   their own at this pace.
2. **The adapter is held between computers.** The phone's log has one
   RFCOMM connect for runs 1 to 3 (17:24:52 to 17:29:42), and after
   each run the relay's line said "The adapter is open; waiting for a
   computer". (The relay was started once before, at 17:24:16, and
   stopped at 17:24:46 before any computer connected.) The owner saw the
   traffic counts rise while a computer was connected.
3. **A second computer is refused, and the first carries on.** Started
   5 s into run 3: `error` code 7, "127.0.0.1:35000: the port failed
   during the adapter's setup", exit 3. Its wire log shows the port
   closed 5 ms after `ATZ` went out. Run 3 had no gap. The message
   doesn't say the relay is busy; the run sheet's suspect list does.
4. **Stop relaying ends the computer's link.** In run 4, a `health`
   line, code 9 "transport hard failure", at 13.9 s, as the phone's log
   shows the RFCOMM socket closed (17:31:51.8). `havne-cli` kept trying
   to connect on its backoff, with nothing listening, until its 120 s.
   The one timeout is the request in flight at the stop.
5. **The foreground service holds the relay.** While relaying, the
   notification read "Relaying elm327://bt:... to a computer" (ongoing,
   not swipeable) and `SessionService` ran in the foreground; after Stop
   relaying both were gone.

## 9b: one DID sweep block

`havne-cli stream
"elm327://127.0.0.1:35000?profile=uds-generic&did=1000-13FF&scan=first&identify=0&diagnose=0"
--until-scanned --seconds 300`, 17:36:12 to 17:36:48:

6. **The block completed in 32.9 s, about 31 DIDs a second.** 1,024
   DIDs on the engine ECU: 4 answered, 1,020 refused (`7F 22 31`), the
   last `scan` line `complete` with `left` 0, and the `end` line's
   reason `scanned`. No frame dropped, no `health` line. This is the
   first run of `scan=first` and the response-count digit for one DID
   ([ADR-0020](../adr/0020-uds-generic.md)'s and ADR-0026's appendices
   of 2026-11-01) on an adapter. No DID was asked again without the
   digit: every reply in the block was one line, and the wire log has
   no "spans frames" line.
7. **The screen was on.** The phone's log shows the display on during
   the block, so 9b's screen-off hour is still to run.

## 7c2: Disconnect while replies arrive

The app on Settings with the MX+'s URI (`profile=obd2`), Record
sessions on; a script on the desktop tapped Connect, waited 14 s (the
connect, then about 10 s of values), tapped Disconnect and waited 6 s,
20 times, 17:55:38 to 18:02:30, checking the app's process after each.

8. **No crash in 20 disconnects.** Twenty RFCOMM connects in the
   phone's log, each followed by a Disconnect while Service 01 replies
   were arriving; no `Fatal signal` or `FATAL EXCEPTION` in the log, and
   the app's process the same one from before the first cycle to after
   the last. The crash the fix addresses came once in eight such
   disconnects.
9. **Every recording ends `end disconnected`.** 20 recordings, 160 to
   359 frames each (the connect's length varies), 0 frames dropped by
   the bridge in any. Each connect read the ECU's supported PIDs and
   its diagnostics before the values.

The crash was seen under `uds-generic`; this run used `obd2`, the run
sheet's URI. Both keep replies arriving at the Disconnect, which is
what the fix is about.
