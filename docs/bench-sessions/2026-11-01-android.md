| 4, Stop relaying at 13.9 s | 433 | (stopped, below) | 1 | 18 / 106 / 142 ms | 31 ms |

1. **A relayed session is an ELM327 on a socket to core.** Each
   `session` line names the MX+'s firmware, protocol 6 at 500 kbit/s and
   the engine ECU on `0x7E8`; no frame was dropped in any run. The rate,
   about 31 requests a second, is the MX+'s own over Bluetooth
   ([ADR-0026](../adr/0026-elm327-transport.md)'s appendix "the first
   vehicle session" of 2026-10-27: ~32), so the relay and `adb forward` add no limit of
   their own at this pace.
2. **The adapter is held between computers.** The phone's log has one
   RFCOMM connect for runs 1 to 3 (17:24:52 to 17:29:42), and after
   each run the relay's line said "The adapter is open; waiting for a
   computer". (The relay was started once before, at 17:24:16, and
   stopped at 17:24:46 before any computer connected.) The owner saw the
   traffic counts rise while a computer was connected.
3. **A second computer is refused, and the first carries on.** Started
   5 s into run 3: `error` code 7, "127.0.0.1:35000: the port failed
   during the adapter's setup", exit 3. Its wire log shows the port
   closed 5 ms after `ATZ` went out. Run 3 had no gap. The message
   doesn't say the relay is busy; the run sheet's suspect list does.
4. **Stop relaying ends the computer's link.** In run 4, a `health`
   line, code 9 "transport hard failure", at 13.9 s, as the phone's log
   shows the RFCOMM socket closed (17:31:51.8). `havne-cli` kept trying
   to connect on its backoff, with nothing listening, until its 120 s.
   The one timeout is the request in flight at the stop.
5. **The foreground service holds the relay.** While relaying, the
   notification read "Relaying elm327://bt:... to a computer" (ongoing,
   not swipeable) and `SessionService` ran in the foreground; after Stop
   relaying both were gone.

## More than one ECU

Through the relay, the engine idling: each of 16 targets asked two
questions for 10 s each, every request read-only. `01 00` (Service 01's
supported-PID bitmap) under `raw` with `?poll=0x00`, and `22 F190` (the
VIN DID) under `uds-generic` with `?did=F190`. The targets: physical
`0x7E0` to `0x7E7` (replies on the ID + 8), then functional `0x7DF`
heard on each of `0x7E8` to `0x7EF` in turn, since the setup's receive
filter (`AT CRA`) passes one reply ID a session.

| Request to reply | `01 00` | `22 F190` | Through `0x7DF` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0x7E0` to `0x7E8` | 315 answers, p50 18 ms, p99 114 ms | 144 answers (the VIN, three frames), p50 57 ms | both answered |
| `0x7E1` to `0x7E9` | 310 answers, p50 18 ms, p99 107 ms | refused once, `7F 22 31` | `01 00` answered; `22 F190` silent |
| `0x7E2` to `0x7E7` | silent (about 80 timeouts each) | silent | silent |

10. **Two ECUs answer Service 01.** The engine ECU, and a second at
    `0x7E1`/`0x7E9` whose bitmap differs (`41 00 98...` against
    `41 00 FE...`); it answers UDS but not F190.
11. **Both answer a functional request.** `01 00` through `0x7DF`
    reached both, so a functional request on this car has two replies.
12. **Functional requests run at half the rate:** 154 and 157 answers
    in 10 s against 310 and 315 physical, p99 147 ms against 107 to
    114. A functional request never takes the response-count digit
    (core can't know how many ECUs answer), so each waits out the
    adapter's `AT ST` timer.
13. **A functional request's refusal is silent.** `0x7E1` refuses
    `22 F190` with `7F 22 31` physically and says nothing to it through
    `0x7DF`. The simulated ECU keeps the same silence, by its reading
    of ISO 14229-1 (`core/src/sim_ecu.h`): the car agrees with it.
14. **A reply that spans frames, under the response-count digit, on an
    adapter.** Asked physically, `22F1901` brought the First Frame
    alone and the prompt: the MX+ counts a First Frame as the one
    response. The transport logged "22 F190's reply spans frames: asked
    again, and with no response count from now on" and asked again
    without the digit, and the whole reply came
    ([ADR-0026](../adr/0026-elm327-transport.md)'s appendix of
    2026-11-01).

Not covered: modules on IDs outside `0x7E0`-`0x7EF` or behind a
gateway, and both ECUs' replies to one functional request in one
session, which needs a receive mask a URI can't ask for yet.

## 9b: the screen off, a sweep of every DID

The engine idling, the relay started at 18:08 and the phone's screen
turned off at 18:27 (Android's `Dozing`); then the engine ECU's whole
DID range in blocks of 1,024 (`0000-FFFF`, 1000-13FF done above), each
`--until-scanned --seconds 120`, 18:27:00 to 19:02:52. The desktop ran
a build and the test suite during part of it.

15. **The relay held for 36 minutes with the screen off.** 63 blocks,
    69,992 frames, no `health` or `error` line in any. At the end the
    screen was still off, `SessionService` in the foreground, its
    partial wake lock held since the relay started (54 minutes), and
    the app's process never frozen: what ADR-0029 holds for a session
    held for the relay.
16. **The sweep read 64,509 DIDs in 36 minutes,** about 30 a second
    with each block's connect: 224 answered, 64,285 refused. With the
    first block, the engine ECU answers 228 DIDs.
17. **Three DIDs were never answered, and that was a bug.** Two blocks
    ran to their 120 s with DIDs left (one, then two). Each was a DID
    whose reply spans many frames: the digit cut it after its First
    Frame, the transport asked again at once without it, and the ECU
    answered NO DATA. Asking one of them by hand through the relay
    (`22 DE00`, a 131-byte reply) showed why: the adapter sends flow
    control after the First Frame and the ECU goes on sending the
    other 18 Consecutive Frames, about 8 ms apart, taking no request
    until they're sent. A request 0 or 50 ms after the cut reply's
    prompt got NO DATA; 150 ms or more, the whole reply. The VIN's
    reply (three frames) was done before its re-ask went out. A
    scanned DID left unanswered waits for a rescan, so the block never
    completed. The fix holds the ECU's requests until a cut reply's
    rest has gone ([ADR-0026](../adr/0026-elm327-transport.md)'s
    appendix of 2026-11-01, amended).
18. **The fix on the car** (19:32-19:34, `havne-cli` from the fix's
    branch, the engine idling): both blocks again, each `scanned` in
    about 35 s with nothing left, D800-DBFF 46 found and DC00-DFFF 4.
    The wire log shows DE00 cut at 19:33:22.938 and asked again at
    23.180, 242 ms later (its 18 frames' hold), and the whole reply
    back; DE01 the same.
