# Android phone session 5: the window after a turn, the fold chevrons, lambda's band

**Date:** 2026-10-28  **Phone:** Android 16 (SDK 36), the test phone
**Adapter:** none
**QtTune build:** e3f0294 (CI run 40758906017), installed over `adb`
after the previous build was uninstalled
**Evidence:** the owner's notes from the phone

Step 1 of [ADR-0028](../adr/0028-app-fed-streams.md), the fifth run,
after [2026-10-28-android-4](2026-10-28-android-4.md): the build with
the window following the screen through a turn, the drawn fold
chevrons, Live's band for a crowded trace's swings with ten trace
colors, and the Record line naming a replay.

## Findings

1. **No bands after a turn.** Upright to landscape and back, the app
   fills the screen.
2. **The fold buttons show their chevrons.**
3. **Lambda reads well** on Live through the limiter.
4. **The Record line** reads "Replays aren't recorded" with a replay in
   the field.
5. **The navigation bar is dark with light buttons.**
6. **A preset after a replay fails to connect.** With a recording's
   replay in the field (`?profile=mock`), a preset of another scheme
   kept the query, so the next connect carried `profile=mock` to the
   adapter. Any typed query carries over the same way, and clearing it
   by hand is the only way out.
7. **The "Connected:" line wraps** with a long URI, the page inside
   the screen.
8. **Settings is cluttered.** The owner asked for the first things a
   user wants at the top (after Connect… on Home, the adapter list sat
   at the bottom of the page), everything folded at first, and headings
   that say what each part is for.

## After the run

- A preset or device row of another scheme keeps only the query keys
  that scheme takes (as core lists them per transport), and the mock
  profile is dropped on an adapter, the profile row showing Default; one
  of the same scheme keeps the query whole (finding 6).
- Settings is the connect bar and five folded sections, Adapter first,
  each heading summing up its section; Connect… elsewhere opens Settings
  with Adapter unfolded (finding 8).
