ICU 73.2, in the AppImage
-------------------------

The AppImage's libicudata, libicui18n and libicuuc files come with the
Qt kit Havne is built with: ICU 73.2, copyright Unicode, Inc. and
others, under the Unicode license in licenses/ICU.txt, which also
lists the third-party data and code ICU includes. Its source is at
https://github.com/unicode-org/icu/releases/tag/release-73-2.


System libraries, in the AppImage
---------------------------------

The AppImage also carries libraries from the Ubuntu release it is
built on, each under its own license; the release's SBOM
(havne-<version>.spdx.json) names each one and its package's version.
Each package's copyright file, as Ubuntu ships it, is
usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright in the AppImage, and the license
texts those files point to in /usr/share/common-licenses are in
common-licenses/ beside this file.


The AppImage runtime, in the AppImage
-------------------------------------

The AppImage begins with the AppImage type 2 runtime, which mounts or
unpacks the rest when it starts:
type2-runtime release 20251108 (commit dd6cebe)
from https://github.com/AppImage/type2-runtime, copyright probonopd and
contributors, under the MIT license in licenses/AppImage-runtime.txt.
The runtime is one static program that also contains:

    libfuse 3.15.0, with the runtime's patch to lib/mount.c, under the
      GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1
      (licenses/LGPL-2.1.txt)
    squashfuse 0.5.2, under its BSD license (licenses/squashfuse.txt)
    musl libc 1.2.5, under the MIT license (licenses/musl.txt)
    mimalloc 2.1.7, under the MIT license (licenses/mimalloc.txt)
    zstd 1.5.6, under the BSD license of its two (licenses/zstd.txt)
    zlib 1.3.1, under the zlib license (licenses/zlib.txt)

libfuse and squashfuse are built from those releases by the runtime's
build scripts; musl, mimalloc, zstd and zlib come from Alpine Linux
3.21's packages. As LGPL-2.1 section 6 asks, the source of libfuse and
of the runtime it is linked into is attached to each Havne release
that carries an AppImage, beside the AppImage itself:
fuse-3.15.0.tar.xz (libfuse's own release) and
type2-runtime-20251108.tar.gz (the runtime at that commit, with its
libfuse patch and build scripts). With them you can rebuild the runtime
against your own libfuse and put it in place of the AppImage's first
part. They are also at https://github.com/libfuse/libfuse/releases/tag/fuse-3.15.0
and https://github.com/AppImage/type2-runtime/releases/tag/20251108.

declares:

    androidx.startup:startup-runtime
    androidx.tracing:tracing

Android NDK, under the Apache License, version 2.0 with LLVM
Exceptions, which ask for no notice in a library's binary form.

NHTSA vPIC manufacturer codes
-----------------------------

The makes Havne shows for a VIN's manufacturer code come from the
standalone database of NHTSA's Product Information Catalog and Vehicle
Listing (vPIC), https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/downloads/, a work of the
US government, whose terms of use call it public information that may
be distributed or copied. The release used is named in Havne's source
(core/src/vin_wmi_table.h). NHTSA gives no warranty of its accuracy,
and Havne is not affiliated with or endorsed by NHTSA.
